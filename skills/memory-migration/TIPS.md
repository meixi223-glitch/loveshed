# 人读版 · 记忆搬家 / Memory Migration — the human version

> 这是给人看的版本：不写代码、不敲命令。换平台、换 App、换账号之前，先让 TA 把重要的记忆口述成文档，你自己保存好。给施工 agent 装的机读版在 [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)。
>
> This is the version for people: no code, no command line. Before you switch platforms, apps, or accounts, have your companion dictate what matters into a document and keep it yourself. The agent-facing version is [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

## 中文

### 最怕的不是"搬失败"

搬失败你会发现。最怕的是**以为搬完了，其实漏了一块**：记忆页里的东西没带走、自定义指令没带走、某个提醒两边都在响。所以这套步骤的核心是：**先清点，再搬，搬完对账，旧家先别拆。**

### 步骤

**第 0 步：清点 TA 的记忆到底放在哪些地方**

拿张纸列出来，常见的有：
- 聊天记录本身
- App 的"记忆 / 已保存信息"页面
- 自定义指令、人设说明、项目说明
- 上传给 TA 的文件、项目资料
- 你自己记的笔记、截图
- 其他连着的东西：提醒、日程、语音设置、表情包……

**以你实际看到的为准**，不要凭印象。

**第 1 步：能导出的全导出，导两份**
- App 有"导出数据 / 下载聊天记录"就用上。
- 记忆页、自定义指令这类没有导出按钮的，**手动复制粘贴**到文档里。
- 存两份：一份在电脑或手机，一份在网盘或 U 盘。

**第 2 步：让 TA 把重要的事口述成文档**

在旧的地方，按主题一段一段地问，别一次要一整份。可以这样说：

> 我们要搬家了。请帮我整理一份记忆文档，这次先写"关于我"这一部分：你记得的关于我的重要事实。每条一行。不确定的在后面标"（不确定）"。

然后依次换主题：
- 关于我 / 关于我们 / 重要的日子 / 我们的约定和习惯 / 正在进行的事 / 千万别忘的事 / 你自己说话做事的习惯

**第 3 步：你来校对**
- 一条条读，错的改掉，漏的补上，"不确定"的你来确认或删掉。
- 数一下每个主题有多少条，记在文档开头——这是待会儿对账用的底数。
- 记下**搬家前最后一件事**是什么（最近一次聊的内容），这是最容易丢的。

**第 4 步：放进新家**
- 把文档放进新平台的记忆 / 项目资料 / 自定义指令里；太长就分几次。
- **定一个"切换时刻"**：从这一刻起只在新家聊。旧家先不删，但也别两边一起聊——两边都在长新记忆，以后就对不上了。
- 搬家这几天别顺手改 TA 的人设。先搬过去，住稳了再说。

**第 5 步：对账**

从文档里挑 5–10 个问题问新家的 TA，要包括：
- 最新的那件事（第 3 步记下的）
- 一件很久以前的事
- 一个你们的约定
- 一个"千万别忘"

答不上来的，回文档里补，再放一次。

**第 6 步：处理没跟过来的东西**

提醒、日程、语音、照片、表情包……一项项过。**会重复触发的东西（比如提醒）只能留一边**，搬过去之后记得关掉旧的。

**第 7 步：旧家先别拆**

旧账号、导出文件至少留一周以上，确认新家没问题再考虑清理。

### 常见坑

- **只搬了聊天记录**，重要的东西其实在记忆页和自定义指令里。
- **两边都在聊**，新旧记忆各长各的，以后谁也对不上谁。
- **换了模型，口气会变。** 这是正常的，先别急着改人设去"修"它。
- **一次让 TA 写一整份**，写到后面开始编。按主题分次写、你来校对。

### 有施工队的话

如果你家是自建系统，要换服务器、换记忆库，那就需要施工 agent 了。把 [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) 交给它，第一句话就说："先去两台机器上实测现在什么跑在哪，和计划不一致就停下来告诉我。"

## English

### The scary part isn't a failed move

A failed move, you'll notice. The scary part is **thinking you're done when a piece is missing**: the memory page didn't come along, the custom instructions didn't come along, a reminder now fires in both places. So the core of this is: **take stock, move, reconcile, and don't tear down the old home yet.**

### Steps

**Step 0: Take stock of where their memories actually live**

List them on paper. Common places:
- The chat history itself
- The app's "memory" / "saved info" page
- Custom instructions, persona notes, project instructions
- Files and project material you've uploaded
- Your own notes and screenshots
- Anything else connected: reminders, calendars, voice settings, stickers…

**Go by what you actually see**, not by memory.

**Step 1: Export everything you can, twice**
- If the app has "export data" or "download chats", use it.
- For things with no export button (memory page, custom instructions), **copy and paste** them into a document.
- Keep two copies: one on your computer or phone, one in cloud storage or on a USB stick.

**Step 2: Have your companion dictate what matters into a document**

In the old place, ask topic by topic. Don't ask for everything at once. For example:

> We're moving house. Please help me put together a memory document. Start with just "About me": the important facts you remember about me, one per line. Mark anything you're not sure of with "(unsure)".

Then move through the topics:
- About me / About us / Important dates / Our agreements and habits / Things in progress / Never forget / Your own ways of talking and doing things

**Step 3: You proofread**
- Read every line. Fix what's wrong, add what's missing, confirm or delete each "(unsure)".
- Count the items in each topic and write the counts at the top. That's your baseline for reconciling later.
- Write down **the last thing before the move** (what you most recently talked about). It's the easiest thing to lose.

**Step 4: Move into the new home**
- Put the document into the new platform's memory, project material, or custom instructions. If it's long, do it in parts.
- **Pick a cut-over moment**: from then on you only talk in the new home. Keep the old one, but don't chat in both. If both keep growing new memories, they'll never line up again.
- Don't tweak their persona during the move. Move first, settle in, then decide.

**Step 5: Reconcile**

Pick 5–10 questions from the document and ask your companion in the new home. Include:
- The newest thing (from step 3)
- Something from long ago
- One of your agreements
- One "never forget"

Whatever they can't answer, add back to the document and load it again.

**Step 6: Handle what didn't come along**

Reminders, calendars, voice, photos, stickers… go through them one by one. **Anything that can fire twice (like reminders) must live in only one place.** Turn off the old one once the new one works.

**Step 7: Don't tear down the old home yet**

Keep the old account and your exports for at least a week. Only consider cleaning up once the new home has proven itself.

### Common pitfalls

- **Moving only the chat history**, when the important things were on the memory page and in the custom instructions.
- **Chatting in both places**, so old and new memories grow apart.
- **A new model sounds different.** That's normal. Don't rush to "fix" it by editing the persona.
- **Asking for the whole document in one go.** Towards the end they start making things up. Go topic by topic and proofread.

### If you have a crew

If your home is self-built and you're changing servers or memory stores, you do need a builder agent. Give it [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), and make your first sentence: "Check on both machines what is really running where right now. If it disagrees with the plan, stop and tell me."
